## Wake word (`hey_jarvis_v0.1`)

- Command: `jarvis bench wake`
- Run: 2026-11-20T23:15:37+00:00 (wall time 337.3 s)
- Hardware: Apple M2 (Mac14,7), 8.0 GB RAM, macOS 15.5, Python 3.12.13
- Positives: 28 English Kokoro voices x 2 phrases ('Hey Jarvis.' / "Hey Jarvis, what's the weather like today?") x speeds 0.85, 1.0, 1.2 = 168 clips per condition. Babble noise is other LibriSpeech speech; SNR is relative to the wake clip's speech power.
- Negatives: LibriSpeech test-clean (all chapters, each concatenated): 87 chapters, 5.4 h. A false accept is a threshold crossing, with a 2 s refractory period.

False-reject rate (lower is better):

| Condition | t = 0.3 | t = 0.5 | t = 0.7 | t = 0.9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 0.0% | 1.2% | 1.2% | 1.2% |
| quiet -20 dB | 0.0% | 1.2% | 1.2% | 1.2% |
| white noise 10 dB SNR | 0.6% | 3.0% | 5.4% | 6.6% |
| speech babble 10 dB SNR | 5.9% | 9.5% | 9.5% | 10.7% |
| speech babble 0 dB SNR | 40.5% | 52.4% | 52.4% | 53.6% |
| **all conditions** | **9.4%** | **13.5%** | **13.9%** | **14.6%** |

False accepts on non-wake speech:

| | t = 0.3 | t = 0.5 | t = 0.7 | t = 0.9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| False accepts | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Per hour | 0.37 | 0.18 | 0.18 | 0.00 |

Voices with the most misses at t = 0.5 (all conditions): `am_onyx` 47%, `af_bella` 27%, `am_echo` 27%, `bf_alice` 27%, `am_santa` 23%

**Limits.** The positives are clean, close-mic synthetic speech, and the wake model itself was trained on synthetic speech (from other TTS engines), so these false-reject rates are a best case: they say nothing about real accents, distance from the mic, room echo or children, and 28 voices is a small speaker sample. LibriSpeech is read audiobook speech; TV, music and conversation will behave differently, so treat the false-accept rate as a lower bound for a noisy home.
